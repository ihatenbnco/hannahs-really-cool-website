Why do I have an OrcaSlicer download?


Bambu Lab, a company that makes 3D printers has some of their code licensed under the GPL, meaning that anyone is free to use and distribute and modify it, but when someone made a slicer called OrcaSlicer, Bambu Lab legally threatened the developer, even though the developer was legally allowed to edit the code and post it to their Github. For more information on it, you can watch Louis Rossman's video on it.
